A native Kindle → Hardcover sync

Read in the Kindle's own reader, the way you always do. Endpaper quietly sends your progress, finished books, ratings and highlights to Hardcover.

Download the latest release View on GitHub

For jailbroken Kindles. Free and open source (MIT).

Why Endpaper

The Kindle only talks to Goodreads. If you track your reading on Hardcover, you either update every book by hand, or you replace the Kindle reader with another app such as KOReader, and give up the reader you like.

Endpaper takes a third path. It is a small companion program that only watches the stock reader. It reads the same files the Kindle already keeps about your books, and sends the changes to Hardcover. Nothing about how you read changes.

It was built to stay out of the way: no new reader, no setup over SSH, no settings to tune, and as little battery and storage use as possible.

What it syncs

Reading progress

Your page is sent when you close the book or the Kindle goes to sleep. Forward only: it never moves your progress back.

New books

Start a book and it is added to Currently Reading, matched by ISBN, ASIN, or title and author.

Finished books

Reach the end and the book is marked Read with today's date.

Re-reads

Start a finished book again and Hardcover gets a new read, not an overwrite.

Star ratings

Rate a book on the Kindle's end-of-book screen and the stars go to Hardcover.

Highlights & notes

Sent as private journal entries on the book. Old ones can be imported in one tap.

How it works

Observes, never changes

Reads the Kindle's reading database and files. The Kindle reader and your books stay untouched.

Event driven

Wakes up when you leave a book, the Kindle sleeps or Wi-Fi returns. No constant polling.

Offline friendly

No Wi-Fi? Changes wait and are sent when you are back online.

One tap to manage

An Endpaper item in your library opens a simple page: sign in with a QR code, status, sync now, check setup.

Install

  1. Download endpaper-<version>.zip from the latest release.
  2. Connect the Kindle with USB and unzip the file onto the Kindle drive. You get documents/Endpaper.sh and an endpaper folder.
  3. Eject, then tap Endpaper in your library.
  4. Tap Sign in, scan the QR code with your phone and approve. Done.

After a restart of the Kindle, tap Endpaper once to start sync again. Full instructions, updating and uninstalling: see the README.

Good to know

  • Requirements: a jailbroken Kindle that runs scriptlets (.sh files in the library), a Hardcover account, and Wi-Fi to sync.
  • Tested on one Kindle with firmware 5.17.1 so far. Reports from other models are welcome on GitHub.
  • Privacy: your sign-in stays on the Kindle. Endpaper talks only to Hardcover, and to GitHub at most once a day to check for updates while its page is open.
  • Independent: Endpaper is not made by or affiliated with Hardcover or Amazon.
